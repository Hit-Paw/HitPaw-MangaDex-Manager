# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a **harassment-free experience for everyone**, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, trolling, or discriminatory comments
- Publishing others’ private information without explicit permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, or sexual attention / advances of any kind
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported:

- **Preferred**: Private security advisory on [GitHub](https://github.com/Hit-Paw/HitPaw-MangaDex-Manager/security/advisories/new)
- **Alternative**: Discord — [discord.gg/z6yYYpcYYc](https://discord.gg/z6yYYpcYYc) (DM a maintainer)

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy of the reporter. Enforcement decisions, reasoning, and any temporary or permanent consequences will be communicated transparently — with respect for those involved.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.

## Enforcement Guidelines

1. **Correction** — Use of inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior → private written warning, apology requested.
2. **Warning** — Violation through a single incident or series → warning with consequences for continued behavior, no interaction with involved people for a period.
3. **Temporary Ban** — Serious violation → temporary ban from community interaction.
4. **Permanent Ban** — Pattern of violation, harassment, or aggression → permanent ban.
